# Photos of the RH page's timeline

Portraits beside the timeline in "Where we stand" on the RH page (Rh.tsx). Each comes from Wikimedia Commons (downloaded
from upload.wikimedia.org); each licence was checked on the file page (public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA only). The
site shows the author and licence with every photo, linked to its Commons page and the licence. Kevin Buzzard's photo is
the Views page's, `views/buzzard.webp` (credited in `public/views/CREDITS.md`). People without a freely licensed photo
on Commons (Carlos Simpson, Vladimir Shchur, Michael Harris, Alessandro Maria Masullo) are shown with initials; Mikhail
Kapranov and Brian Conrad, also named in the entries, have none either and are not shown.
