# Photos of the Views page

Portraits of the people quoted from 3Blue1Brown's video "What if AI solved the Riemann Hypothesis?". None is taken from
the video. Each comes from Wikimedia Commons (downloaded from upload.wikimedia.org); each licence was checked on the
file page (public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA only). The site shows the author and licence with every photo, linked
to its Commons page and the licence. People without a freely licensed photo on Commons (Elliot Glazer, Daniel Litt,
Adam Brown, Johannes Schmitt, Ankur Moitra, Jared Duker Lichtman) are shown with initials.
