# Pictures of the History page

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons; each licence was checked on the file page (public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA
only). The site shows the author and licence under every photo, linked to its Commons page. Cards without a freely
licensed photo (Mizar, LCF, Lean, mathlib, Lean 4, the Equational Theories Project, AlphaProof, Formal Conjectures,
IMO 2025, Comparator, the Navier–Stokes certificates) use SVG drawings made for this site (Apache-2.0, like the code).
